STARTER OPTIONS

A practical place to start

For new relationships, a focused engagement can establish priorities and demonstrate how we work before either side commits to something larger.

01

MarTech or Analytics Audit

Review the current stack, implementation or measurement setup; identify gaps and prioritise actions that can improve reliability, adoption or performance.

02

CRM & Automation Health Check

Assess platform configuration, data flows, journeys and operating practices; recommend practical improvements and next steps.

03

AI Use-Case Sprint

A focused, typically two-to-four-week engagement to evaluate a business use case, examine feasibility and define or demonstrate a workable next step. Scope and deliverables are agreed before starting.

ENGAGEMENT TYPES

Choose the relationship that fits

01

Consulting & Advisory

For decisions that need clarity before investment or execution. Engage us for assessments, strategy, platform selection, architecture, roadmaps or solution blueprints.

02

Implementation & Project Delivery

For a defined capability that needs to be built, integrated, migrated or launched. We can deliver an agreed project from end to end or take over at a suitable stage.

03

Managed Services

For teams that need ongoing platform, campaign or solution operations and continuous improvement. We can operate what we built or take responsibility for an existing environment.

04

Specialist Talent & Managed Capacity

For teams needing niche technical or operational expertise. Access a dedicated specialist, a project team or short-term expert assistance with ClarioServ oversight rather than a generic staffing arrangement.

COMMERCIAL MODELS

Choose the commercial structure

01

Fixed Price / Fixed Scope

Best suited to a clearly defined set of deliverables, assumptions and acceptance criteria, such as an audit, blueprint or bounded implementation.

02

Time & Materials

Useful when priorities may evolve or the full scope cannot be responsibly fixed upfront. Work is billed against agreed effort and rates.

03

Dedicated FTE / Capacity

A practical fit for sustained access to specialist skills, ongoing delivery backlogs or an extended team working alongside your own people.

04

Retainer / Managed-Service Model

Suitable for recurring operations, campaign execution, platform support and optimisation within an agreed service scope.

The engagement and pricing model are separate choices. We will recommend a structure that fits the scope, pace and level of ongoing support you need.

DELIVERY OPTIONS

Work across locations

Engagements can be delivered remotely, onsite at your location or through a hybrid arrangement. For international clients, India-based delivery can be organised with working-hour overlap suited to the client team and engagement needs.
