THE DELIVERY LIFECYCLE

Four stages. One continuous cycle.

Each stage has a clear purpose and tangible outputs. Learning from operation feeds back into the next decision, so the work keeps improving.

01

Clarify: Understand what needs to change

We start by understanding the business objective, the customer or operational problem, the technology landscape and the constraints. Where a programme is already underway, we assess only what is needed to make the next decision confidently.

Together, we identify priorities, assumptions, dependencies and what meaningful progress would look like. The output is a shared understanding of the problem, the scope and the decisions needed to move ahead.

02

Blueprint: Make the plan executable

We translate the agreed priorities into a solution and delivery plan. Depending on the initiative, this may include experience and journey design, architecture, platform choices, integration requirements, data and measurement design, delivery sequencing and resource needs.

The output is a practical blueprint: clear enough to guide implementation, realistic enough to reflect constraints and focused on the result the business wants.

03

Build: Turn the plan into a working system

We configure, develop, integrate, migrate and test the agreed solution. Business and technical teams stay connected as decisions arise, so implementation does not drift away from the original intent.

The output is a launched or production-ready capability, supported by the documentation and operational knowledge needed to use and maintain it.

04

Run & Optimise: Make the investment work harder

A launch is a milestone, not the finish line. We support operations, monitor performance, resolve issues and identify improvements in adoption, efficiency and customer or business outcomes.

The learning from this stage feeds the next round of Clarify. Priorities evolve, new use cases emerge and the solution improves with them.

05

Learn, refine and return to Clarify

Insights from Run & Optimise shape the next priorities. This is the feedback loop, not an additional delivery stage.

↶

The loop continues: Run & Optimise → Clarify.
