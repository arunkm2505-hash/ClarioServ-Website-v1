FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers about what we do, where we work and how engagements are set up.

What does ClarioServ do?

ClarioServ is a Chennai-based digital, MarTech, data and AI services company. We help organisations plan, implement and keep improving their customer, marketing and data platforms, from strategy and advisory through implementation, campaign operations, managed services and specialist talent, as one accountable partner.

Where is ClarioServ based, and do you work with international clients?

We’re based in Chennai, India. We work remotely, onsite or in a hybrid model, and for international clients we arrange working-hour overlap that suits your team.

How does an engagement usually start?

Most new relationships begin with a focused engagement: a MarTech or analytics audit, a CRM and automation health check, or an AI use-case sprint, which typically takes two to four weeks. We agree the scope, timeline and price before work begins.

Which platforms do you work with?

Our experience covers Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Data Cloud, Adobe Experience Cloud, HubSpot, Zoho, MoEngage, CleverTap and WebEngage, as well as GA4, Google Tag Manager, Adobe Analytics, Looker Studio, Power BI and customer data platforms. We start from your requirements rather than forcing a predetermined stack.

Can you take over an existing platform or a half-finished implementation?

Yes. We can join at the stage you need: review an existing plan, close the gaps in an implementation that’s underway, or take on operations and optimisation for a platform someone else built.

How is pricing structured?

You can choose fixed price for a clearly defined set of deliverables, time and materials when the scope may evolve, dedicated FTE capacity for sustained specialist support, or a retainer for ongoing managed services. We recommend the structure that fits the scope, pace and level of support you need.
